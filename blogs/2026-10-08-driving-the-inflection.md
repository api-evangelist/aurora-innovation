Transformative technologies develop for years before they reach a decisive commercial inflection point. The car, the shipping container, and the internet each spent two decades or more in quiet incubation before they suddenly seemed inevitable. At our 2026 Analyst & Investor Day in Dallas, we made the case that autonomous trucking, and Aurora, have arrived at that moment.
