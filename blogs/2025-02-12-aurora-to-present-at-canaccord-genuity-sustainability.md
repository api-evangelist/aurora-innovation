PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Aurora Innovation, Inc. (NASDAQ: AUR) today announced David Maday, Chief Financial Officer, will participate in a virtual fireside chat at the Canaccord Genuity Sustainability Summit on February 26, 2025 at 3:10 pm Eastern Time. The presentation will be webcast on Aurora’s investor relations website at ir.aurora.tech.
